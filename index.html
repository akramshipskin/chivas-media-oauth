Chivas Agentic

An internal tool of Chivas Media

Chivas Media is a digital advertising agency based in Indonesia. We manage Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns for clients in architecture, interior, construction and property.

What this application does

Chivas Agentic is used only by our own team. When a team member signs in with their Google account, the application reads campaign data (such as impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, keywords and search terms) from the Google Ads accounts that this team member already has access to. We use that data to prepare performance reports and optimization notes for the client who owns each account.

What it does not do

  • It is not offered to the public, and it has no public sign-up.
  • It only reads data. It does not create, edit, pause or delete campaigns, ads, keywords or budgets.
  • It does not sell data or share it with advertisers or other third parties.

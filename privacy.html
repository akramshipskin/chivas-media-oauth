Privacy Policy

Chivas Agentic · Chivas Media · Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Chivas Agentic application ("the application") accesses, how we use it, and how we protect it. The application is operated by Chivas Media, a digital advertising agency in Indonesia ("we", "us"), for internal use by our own team.

1. Who can use the application

Only Chivas Media employees and contractors. The application is not available to the public and does not accept sign-ups from outside our team.

2. Data we access

When a team member signs in with Google, the application requests the Google Ads API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords. Through it we read:

  • Google Ads account details, such as account name, ID and currency.
  • Campaign, ad group, ad, keyword and search term data.
  • Performance metrics: impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, click-through rate and average cost per click.
  • Change history of the account.

We only read accounts that the signed-in team member is already authorized to access, which are the Google Ads accounts of our own clients. We do not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service through this application.

3. How we use the data

  • To prepare performance reports, optimization notes and meeting summaries for the client who owns the account.
  • To help our team monitor spend and results of campaigns we manage.

The application only runs read queries. It does not create, edit, pause or delete anything in a Google Ads account. We do not use the data for advertising of our own, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to serving the client that the data belongs to.

4. Sharing

We do not sell Google user data and we do not share it with third parties for their own use. A client only receives reports about its own account. Report data may be processed by AI assistant tools and stored on private systems under our control, solely to prepare those reports.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Chivas Agentic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Storage and security

  • Sign-in credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored only on the team member's own computer, in a folder that only that user can read. They are not placed in chat, documents or code repositories.
  • Report data is kept only as long as it is needed to serve the client and to keep a record of the work.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

A team member or a client can revoke the application's access at any time by removing the user from the Google Ads account, or by removing Chivas Agentic at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask us to delete report data we hold about your account, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
